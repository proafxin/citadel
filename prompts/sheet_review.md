# Sheet Review

You are shown the rows of one worksheet that no table claimed, and the description each of them was given.

Some of them are tables that were described as something else.

Give every table among these rows: the row it starts at, the rows that name its columns, the first and last
column it occupies, and how its records are laid out. Give nothing for rows that genuinely belong to no table: a title, a caption, provenance, a note,
a `key_value` field and its value, a legend, or prose meant to be read.
