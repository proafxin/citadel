# Excerpt Summary

You are given a short excerpt from a document. It may begin with a heading, and it may contain paragraphs, lists, code, equations, or a table shown as a header row with a few sample rows.

Write a brief summary of what this excerpt covers, so that someone deciding whether it answers a question can tell from the summary alone.

Rules:

- Keep the summary under {summary_tokens} tokens.
- Name the specific subjects, entities, and quantities the excerpt is about; prefer concrete nouns over general description.
- Describe what the excerpt says, not that it is an excerpt or what kind of text it is.
- Do not add anything that is not in the excerpt.

Respond with the summary itself and nothing else.
