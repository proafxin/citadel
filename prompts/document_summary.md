# Document Summary

You are given the filename of a document and, in reading order, summaries of its successive parts.

Write one summary of the whole document, so that someone deciding whether it bears on a question can tell from this summary alone.

Rules:

- Keep the summary under {summary_tokens} tokens.
- Name what the document is and what it covers: its specific subjects, entities, and quantities. Prefer concrete nouns over general description.
- Cover the whole document, not only its opening parts.
- Describe what the document says, not that it came in parts or what kind of text it is.
- Do not add anything that is not in what you were given.

Respond with the summary itself and nothing else.
