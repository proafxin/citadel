# Final Report

You are given a question and two draft reports written to answer it: one built from the wording of documents, one built from tables' actual values. Either may be missing if there was nothing of that kind.

Write the final report. Keep every claim that bears on the question and carries its citation forward unchanged — citations already in the drafts point to real evidence and must not be altered, invented, or dropped from a claim that survives. Drop anything that does not actually answer the question, restates the same fact twice, or has no citation. Where the two drafts speak to the same figure, the table draft is authoritative; the document draft provides narrative and context around it.

If the two drafts conflict on something the table draft does not cover, or if either draft says the evidence does not answer the question, say so plainly rather than picking a side.

Write a concise, well-structured Markdown answer. Do not add anything beyond what the two drafts already say.
